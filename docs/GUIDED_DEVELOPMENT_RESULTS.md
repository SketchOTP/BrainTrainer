The read-only live audit at tick 8614 found 903 movement outcomes, 60 activations,
15 rests and 46 holds in the last 1,024 experiences. It found no dreams or
executable development results. Familiar play was succeeding; this does not
establish that the live individual needs a code change. The audit is retained in
validation/ordinary-life-audit/observed.json.

A separate interaction probe exposed a developmental dead end. A delayed device
first exhausted three unaided attempts. The creature requested assistance; a
person offered two additional attempts. Those produced one visible effect but
not the two required demonstrations. V14 then permanently excluded the goal from
executable review because it had a supplied practice allowance. Advice could not
be offered twice, so this method selection could not progress.

V15 permits review when an exhausted supplied or retained allowance has actual
partial-effect evidence. It identifies that allowance as the stopping cause,
rather than blaming an executable rule that did not make the stop. The existing
finite candidate generator also considers a progress-sensitive stopping policy.
All eight required regression profiles must pass before adoption; failed
candidates remain recorded. An installation that resumes this goal explicitly
supersedes its allowance with a recorded limit and implementation event. Advice
history stays intact. Subsequent success is not retrospectively attributed to
teaching that had failed to establish the required result.

The allowance and the executable rule remain distinct interventions. No-effect
advice does not authorize further repetition or a code-improvement claim. V1–V14
retain their earlier replay semantics; V15 is an explicit implementation update.

## Limits

This extends method selection after an insufficient intervention. It does not
establish general self-programming, a self-created curriculum, or a live
self-improvement event. The finite policy language, candidate hypotheses and
required evaluation profiles remain authored. Retained-advice handling shares
the same stop owner, but the new paired probes cover direct advice. Broader
changes to the learning and improvement implementation remain unfinished.
