# Proposed development protocol: in-contact gene-7 tract ablation

This is a prospective **development-only** protocol, not yet frozen or executable as a qualified preference evaluation. It compares ordinary execution with the specifically scoped gene-7 persistent-tract-state freeze, not with a whole-brain learning-disabled controller. The freeze's bounded replay qualification and the retained-state ambiguity are described in [Food movement and learning-control investigation](FOOD_MOVEMENT_AND_CONTROL.md). This protocol does not authorize a confirmatory claim or use of the reserved seeds 56–95. Calibrate the lifecycle timing first, then freeze the selected budget, exact runner, source and program digests in a machine-readable manifest before running the preference experiment; record deviations separately and retain raw data.

## Task and assignments

Use `LifecycleFoodExperiment` and `LifecycleDriveCombFrozenExperiment` with the existing preference geometry: two visible, distinguishable foods each 0.5 units away and in contact. This experimental action lifecycle is not established native parity. Run both walking and swimming adapters, seeds 0–15 (development only), both mirrored layouts, and both assignments of food type to positive/negative energy outcome for every seed. Pair the ordinary and frozen conditions at the same initial brain seed, body, mirror and assignment. Each condition receives its own actual outcome feedback and maintains its own environment. Do not provide a preferred target, action or food value to the brain. Check equality of observations and choices at the first tick in each pair, and check that both food categories and actions are available. Any forced accessibility checks must be separately labeled and excluded from behavioral outcomes.

For each paired run allow four sequential phases of 20 opportunities: initial experience, initial evaluation, reversal experience, reversal evaluation. Reverse the mapping at the start of reversal experience. The previous 16-tick in-contact threshold was measured with the old immediate-contact adapter and is not qualified for this lifecycle. Recalibrate budgets 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32 on development seeds 0–15, both bodies, both mirrors and both ordinary/frozen conditions, counting seeds that complete at least one consumption within 20 opportunities. Freeze the shortest budget reaching at least 13/16 in every body/mirror/control cell; if none reaches it, stop before the preference evaluation and report the bottleneck. A non-choice, non-eat action, missing target or failure to complete counts as unsuccessful in the per-opportunity success denominator. Record every observation, attention/target, action, attempted interaction, completion, outcome, inputs, queues and tract state. Save elapsed and CPU time, peak memory with measurement scope, steps and interaction counts. Record full per-run histories and checkpoints; no pooling across mirrored or counterbalanced branches as independent seeds.

`scripts/calibrate_lifecycle_twenty_opportunities.py` implements this calibration and retains censored cases and per-step histories. Its optional smaller development prefix is diagnostic only and cannot select a budget. The full [development calibration](../runs/food-qualification/lifecycle-twenty-calibration-v1.json) selected 32 ticks; its source and program digests must match before the protocol can be frozen. The separate first-opportunity diagnostic is a different measure and cannot substitute for this twenty-opportunity gate.

## In-contact preference, retention and needs

Primary descriptive endpoints by phase and body are the fraction of all opportunities ending in consumption of the currently higher-value food, the fraction completed, target/attention counts, and eat-action counts. Report conditional-on-consumption higher-value rates separately as selected-subset diagnostics. Report the paired ordinary-minus-freeze seed-level difference after first averaging each seed's four mirror/assignment branches, with seed values and variation. The ordinary branch can still learn during evaluation; these are online outcomes, not a read-only retained preference measurement. Do not present a difference as a global learning effect or use the original whole-brain-control acceptance threshold.

After the final experience opportunity and before its evaluation phase, make an independent clone of each branch's complete checkpoint for a **matched-needs probe**. Create another clone after reversal experience. Preserve the original continuation branches. In both probe clones set observable energy to 0.5 and hunger to 0.8, set chemical 150 to 0.8 before the next tick, and set identical contact geometry, food categories and positions for that pair. The fresh probe episode emits no food outcome and does not execute or queue an interaction, so its next brain tick receives no nutritional feedback. Clear pending food outcomes in the probe only and explicitly report this intervention; do not clear neuron values, queues, other chemistry, brain RNG or any other retained state. Record the immediate attention, target and action from the first tick as the matched-needs probe endpoint. Both foods remain within reach, but this probe is a choice observation rather than a completed consumption. Other physiological/neural state may differ, so matching these external needs alone cannot isolate a learned preference from all transient effects.

Keep the ordinary and frozen condition active in their respective probe clones. Verify that non-probe continuations are unchanged by cloning, and that restoring each probe from its JSON checkpoint yields the same initial probe choice and entire ensuing brain state. Also retain an unmodified-needs probe from each checkpoint to show sensitivity to the needs intervention; report both probes, the changes applied and any differing choices. Do not attribute a probe difference to food preference solely from this normalization.

## Qualification checks and decision boundary

Before summarizing results, verify that all ordinary/frozen pairs have equal initial states, that the frozen gene-7 dendrites, weak queue and helper stay equal to their paired initial values, and that ordinary runs are allowed to change them. Check outcome attribution by including separately labeled unrelated and target-mismatched events; these must not be credited to the chosen action. Compare uninterrupted with JSON-checkpointed continuation at a specified midpoint for each condition, requiring identical subsequent choices, outcomes, weak queues and brain state. An exception, control not reapplied after restore, missing raw history or mismatch invalidates the affected comparison and must be reported.

Summarize seed-level learning curves, completed interactions, initial and reversal evaluations and both probe variants; report sparse seeds and zero-completion cases. Any apparent effect is exploratory because this control leaves other learning pathways active and existing development seeds informed the design. A later, separately frozen protocol and independent seeds are required for confirmatory claims; the previously reserved 56–95 remain untouched until the prerequisites of the original qualification protocol are satisfied.
