# Ordinary rural life and developmental opportunities

This audit checks the gap between controlled capability demonstrations and the
continuing individual's ordinary life. It does not declare end-goal completion.
Evidence lives in `validation/ordinary-development-audit/`.

## Read-only live observation

The source checkpoint at tick 16644 preserves the same research individual and
branch. Its latest 1,024 experiences contained 476 world exploration choices,
492 practice approaches, 12 practice interactions, 20 reserve-restoring choices,
and 24 deferrals of repeatedly unavailable actions. Its entire current review
reported zero dreams and zero executable development evaluations. These counts
are not an assertion that the capabilities are absent from the implementation;
they show that this individual's history has not exercised them.

The implementation explains a material disconnect: automatic `rehearsal_due`
looks only at adverse-outcome avoidance goals marked `needs_review`. Curriculum
practice failures and world exploration reviews have separate owners and do not
request this rehearsal. Merely scheduling cosmetic dreams would not fix this:
a rehearsal needs an explicit hypothesis, evidence, a decision it can change,
and later comparison with actual experience.

## Ordinary opportunity branch

A recorded test branch received one nearby delayed-response lantern (four presses
per visible response), using the same body and existing interaction mechanisms.
No action sequence was commanded. Across 256 moments it independently discovered
an unfamiliar affordance, approached it, tried three interactions, stopped with
zero observed effects, and issued one grounded assistance request. It did not
rehearse or request executable improvement. Its executable stopping rule requires
three attempts; the improvement request requires at least one prior observed
effect. This excludes the very first discovery of a slower interaction.

That exclusion is not proof the lantern is useless. Nor would hidden knowledge
of its configured delay justify teaching the creature that it will work. The
next development target is an evidence-grounded investigation of unresolved
activities: distinguish a failed bounded trial from a demonstrated impossibility,
compare limited further practice against costs and other activities, retain
negative as well as positive results, and make re-investigation conditional on
uncertainty and experience. Evaluate against both inert and delayed objects;
never define success as endlessly extending the practice limit.

response to the creature's own request. This is explicitly supplied information,
not autonomous discovery and not a live human interaction. The probe checks
