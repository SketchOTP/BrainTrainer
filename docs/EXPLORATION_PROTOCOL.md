# Persistent exploration qualification

V11 ordinary-life evidence contains substantial movement without a persistent
exploration objective. V12 will learn the signed displacement of available movement
actions from attributed real motion, retain visited position bounds, and choose
short extensions beyond those bounds. It must not read the world's hard limits or
infer a physical obstacle from lack of movement alone.

A goal persists across ticks, restoration and temporary higher-priority activity.
Repeated no-motion results stop that approach and allow a different direction;
blocked directions can be reconsidered after an authored interval. New learning,
useful resources and maintenance take priority. Familiar play may wait for an
already active exploration goal, then resume. Quiet moments remain legitimate.

Prospective seeds 161–164, empty visible environment, 320 ticks each: compare v11
with v12 from matching initial prepared states. Require learned motion evidence,
self-initiated exploration goals, meaningful expansion of visited space, bounded
repeated failed motion at the room edge, both-direction investigation, and exact
restoration. Record movement/quiet time and costs rather than treating coverage as
whole-companion success. Also test zero movement capability, recovery priority,
interruption/resumption, and retained familiar skill after exploration. Current
movement effects must override old predictions when the body changes.
