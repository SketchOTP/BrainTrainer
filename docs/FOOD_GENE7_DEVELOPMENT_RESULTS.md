# In-contact gene-7 development comparison

This is an exploratory, paired development experiment of the [frozen protocol](FOOD_GENE7_CONTACT_PROTOCOL.md), using the optional [body action lifecycle](FOOD_ACTION_LIFECYCLE.md). It is not a whole-brain learning-disabled comparison, native action parity, or a held-out confirmation. Its control executes the gene-7 drive-to-combination tract on temporary state each tick and discards that tract's persistent changes; other tracts, neuron state, feedback and chemistry remain active.

The 32-tick in-contact timing gate passed before the [manifest](../runs/food-qualification/gene7-contact-development-v1-manifest.json) was frozen. Sixteen development seeds were run in both embodiments, with both mirror orientations and both food-value assignments, for 256 branches. Each branch had four phases of 20 opportunities; non-completions are failures in the success denominator. Each body/control/phase cell contains 1,280 opportunities across 16 seeds. The paired percentile bootstrap intervals below resample the 16 seeds after averaging each seed's four mirror/assignment branches (10,000 resamples); these are descriptive development intervals.

| Body and phase | Ordinary success | Gene-7 frozen success | Paired difference | 95% interval |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Walking initial evaluation | 27.3% | 13.5% | +13.8 pp | +10.0 to +17.7 pp |
| Walking reversal evaluation | 22.7% | 14.8% | +8.0 pp | +3.4 to +12.6 pp |
| Swimming initial evaluation | 25.4% | 12.5% | +12.9 pp | +9.1 to +17.0 pp |
| Swimming reversal evaluation | 20.8% | 14.1% | +6.6 pp | +2.9 to +10.5 pp |

Both initial differences exceed ten percentage points in this development sample; neither reversal difference does. The previously specified whole-brain-control/held-out acceptance criteria do not apply to this scoped development ablation. The evidence supports a contribution of persistent gene-7 tract state to the measured behavioral difference under this setup, but does not by itself separate learned food preference from transient feedback, retained neuron activity, execution differences or initial bias. Condition trajectories and the feedback they generate diverge after training starts.

As selected-subset diagnostics, the fraction of completed initial-evaluation consumptions that used the higher-value food was 349/413 for ordinary versus 173/346 for frozen walking, and 325/386 versus 160/320 for swimming. In reversal evaluation it was 291/350 versus 189/378 for walking, and 266/327 versus 181/362 for swimming. These conditional rates exclude failures and must not replace the primary scores.

At the post-experience matched-needs probe, ordinary/frozen selected eating the higher-value target in 43/64 versus 10/64 walking branches, and 46/64 versus 10/64 swimming branches. After reversal experience the corresponding figures were 27/64 versus 8/64 and 33/64 versus 12/64. Each probe clones a checkpoint, removes pending outcome feedback in the clone, fixes geometry and observed needs, and records one brain tick without executing the action. Other retained activity, chemistry and RNG are not normalized, so these are retention-sensitive choice observations, not proof of a stored preference. The raw analysis also records unmodified-needs probes and per-opportunity curves.

The original run received SIGTERM after 237 cases for an unestablished reason. All 237 retained cases were validated; the missing 19 were run separately with the same frozen `case` function and recorded in a [recovery receipt](../runs/food-qualification/gene7-contact-development-v1-recovery.json). The [combined analysis](../runs/food-qualification/gene7-contact-development-v1-recovered-analysis.json) validates all 256 unique keys and 80 opportunities per case, hashes both source files, compares each full paired initial checkpoint excluding the intentional control marker, and checks recorded target-tract deltas and initial/final tract state. Producer per-case midpoint continuation checks passed for all cases; the combined analyzer did not independently replay those continuations. The producer did not record a pre-interruption raw-file digest; recovery and analysis hashes establish the retained files at their respective later audit times, not a prior cryptographic binding. The unrelated and target-mismatched outcome checks exercise the attribution helper, not separately injected end-to-end environment events.

The two runs used approximately 31,431 summed case CPU seconds. Wall time across interruption and recovery and total process-family peak memory were not measured as a single quantity; the largest cumulative worker peak was 314,356 KiB. A separate [native follow-up](../research/docking-station-20261103/brain-connections-20261106/approach-touch-followup/REPORT.md) observed the original engine's cue-assisted approach and touch, but not decision-driven interruption parity. Fresh held-out seeds 56–95 were not used.

The final local `unittest` discovery suite passed 259 tests. Recovery guard tests validate duplicate and truncated rows; synthetic tests exercise the interrupted-run analysis. These tests do not replace native parity or independent replay of the experiment's checkpoint continuations.
