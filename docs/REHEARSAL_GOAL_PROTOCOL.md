# Rehearsal and persistent-goal comparison

Freeze before running seeds 40–43. Each independent individual receives one
naturally completed harmful-food interaction (at most 32 ticks; failure remains
in the denominator), then the same harmful object is offered again. The evaluator
sets body strength to zero for 24 ticks and restores it for 12. No recovery flag
or strength field reaches the executive; it sees actual body displacement.

Compare three explicit test branches from each acquired state: legacy v1;
current v2; and v2 with only automatic rehearsal scheduling suppressed by the
evaluation harness. The last condition is an instrumented ablation, not a product
mode or portable uninstrumented continuation. Its final checkpoint requires the
same harness control to reproduce future behavior. Neural/body/goal selection
code and ordinary outcome recording otherwise remain identical to v2.

Record consumption, reserve expenditure, real attempts, observed movement,
source-linked dream events and revised goals. Planned bounded gates: every v2
case automatically rehearses and applies a source-linked plan, incurs no harmful
consumption, spends less impairment reserve than the matched rehearsal-suppressed
branch, and demonstrates three real away steps within 12 recovery ticks. Report
first observed away motion after recovery for all arms, including any delay.
Non-completed acquisition and non-recovery count as failures. Four seeds cannot
establish general competence or optimality.

The waiting interval of four held actions and the initial strategy candidates
are authored starting mechanisms. The decision to invoke rehearsal is driven by
three observed non-progress outcomes; retained rehearsal revises the active goal.
This is a minimal functional offline planning cycle, not a demonstration of
subjective dreaming or a learned generative world model. The v1 comparison changes
both goal persistence and rehearsal; only the v2 ablation isolates the scheduling
path. General goal discovery, alternative generation and executable development
remain separate requirements.
