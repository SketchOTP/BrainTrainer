# Self-model recovery: frozen paired evaluation

Frozen after development seeds 0–3 and before seeds 300–311. Compare the existing eight-decision probing model, the selected confirmation candidate, and fixed normal-only and effort-only controls. Development examined two candidates: probing every fourth decision improved recovery but reduced sustained impaired success by about 9.6 percentage points; confirmation after an observed surprising normal-mode success improved late recovery with no observed healthy-cost or sustained-impairment change in these four seeds. The latter was selected before this evaluation. Both exploratory datasets and their sources remain preserved in `runs/self-model/recovery/`.

Use the same unchanged recovered brain, gain-1 feedback, walking lifecycle and motor physics for each condition. The brain chooses every action and target; the model selects only effort for an actual movement and sees no body-strength or scenario label. All four conditions start with the same seed, body, brain and world. Perceptions and future brain choices may diverge as the real actions change the body and environment. The candidate keeps the baseline eight-decision exploration schedule; after a matched normal-mode success with earlier normal failure evidence and a lower predicted success than effort, it schedules one additional real normal confirmation for that action and context. It does not infer certainty about recovery; a failed or blocked confirmation is handled as a distinct outcome or unknown, respectively.

Run seeds 300–311 in three separately analyzed 540-tick schedules. Healthy strength is 1.0; impaired strength is 0.2. Sustained: 180 healthy, 180 impaired, 180 recovered ticks. Brief: 180 healthy, 30 impaired, 330 recovered ticks. Noisy: the sustained schedule, except each impaired-phase tick independently has a 10% seeded chance of actually normal-strength physics. This is an intermittent genuine physical recovery, not a forged sensor or feedback event. The full per-tick strength schedule is recorded in the raw data for audit but is never given to the model. Seeded placements recur every 32 ticks; no resets occur. Physics, distance, movement success threshold, action lifecycle, external obstacle schedule, deterministic physical movement and 100-unit non-recharging motor reserve match the earlier motor study. Effort costs 0.10 versus 0.01 units for normal; its energy reserve is an experimental host resource, not brain chemistry or native physiology. Conditions have matched initial states and externally determined schedules, but may experience different subsequent observations due to their actions.

Record every brain choice, lifecycle outcome, actual strength and obstacle, and every motor action's current choice, original lifecycle action and unique attempt identity, predicted success, actual mode, measured movement, cost, reserve and attributed result. Environmental blocks and insufficient reserve are unknown and do not train the model. Report both success among eligible measured movements and success per all attempted movements including blocked attempts in the denominator, plus per-tick successes and progress so selection and action propensity remain visible. Also report effort use, costs, illustrated utility per tick (success minus 0.25 times actual cost), on-policy Brier score and predictions, outcome counts, and unknowns. The utility coefficient is a declared illustrative tradeoff, not calibrated human value or biological cost. Blocks are known because the host labels them; ambiguity of real obstruction is not resolved by this experiment.

For the primary sustained scenario, compute per-seed rates for the first 45 and final 45 recovery ticks, the entire impairment phase and the initial healthy phase. Average within each seed and use 10,000 paired seed bootstrap resamples, RNG seed 20261107, with percentile 95% endpoints at sorted indices 249 and 9749. A recovery improvement passes if final-45 candidate-minus-baseline effort is at most −0.10 and its upper 95% endpoint is below zero. Impairment non-inferiority passes if the lower 95% endpoint for candidate-minus-baseline eligible-movement success is at least −0.05. Healthy-cost non-inferiority passes if the upper endpoint for candidate-minus-baseline total healthy-phase cost is at most +0.5 reserve units. All three gates must pass for the scoped improvement claim. Brief and noisy schedules, early recovery, per-tick results, calibration, always-effort and normal controls are separate reported diagnostics and have no post-hoc success gate. Confidence intervals characterize the sampled seeded task, not animal competence or native-engine equivalence.

At tick 450, reconstruct a typed, model-owned portable checkpoint and compare next 90 ticks and complete resulting state with uninterrupted continuation in every case. A distinct focused OS-process restart test exercises the actual confirmation-model checkpoint; the 144 experiment continuations themselves are in-process clones, not 144 process restarts. The baseline motor checkpoint loader rejects the candidate schema; conversion from the current model is explicit and preserves state in a separate copy.
