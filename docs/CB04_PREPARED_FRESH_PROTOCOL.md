# CB-04 prepared fresh comparison: conditional preregistration

This protocol is eligible to run only after both [development feasibility gates](CB04_PREPARED_DEVELOPMENT_PROTOCOL.md) pass. The prior CB-02 unprepared calibration remains failed and is not reinterpreted. The population here consists of inherited-instinct-prepared creatures, not a native sleep demonstration.

Use 16 fresh seed IDs 500–515, outside documented development, food, motor and recovery seed sets; a bounded scan for explicit seed fields in `runs` and `docs` files under 4 MB found no matches, not a guarantee of global uniqueness. For each seed, construct an adaptive walking individual, apply the declared profile once, then clone matched brain/body/RNG/chemistry/queue/clock/receipt state into all four controller conditions and both schedules. Check equality before each branch. The two layouts are balanced across seeds by parity (eight per layout); both food types are offered in alternating scheduled opportunities. This yields 16 × 2 schedules × 4 conditions = 128 continuous histories, not independent mirror duplications.

Each history uses 24 sequential opportunities: 8 healthy, 8 impaired, 8 recovered. Within each opportunity offer one food at distance 2.0, with initial hunger 0.8, retain evolved body, neural state, motor reserve and clock across opportunities, and allow 64 ticks. Nothing resets learned state or needs between opportunities. Count every scheduled opportunity including no request, unsupported action, interruption, known block, reserve exhaustion or timeout. The existing host ends an opportunity on actual consumption or budget expiration, cancels an unfinished action at timeout and records that cancellation. It does not choose a target or neural action. Sustained impairment applies body strength 0.2 throughout its impaired phase; the intermittent schedule has a seeded 10% chance of genuinely normal-strength physics per impaired tick. The model does not receive strength/schedule labels. Costs are total measured motor-reserve expenditure for all actions during each opportunity, including failed, interrupted, unrelated and exploratory movement. Unsupported choices and measured motor failures are reported separately. Record start geometry, target distance at first eat intent, every observation/choice, lifecycle state, target, event, strength, motor outcome, cost and pending feedback.

For each schedule and phase, average each seed's 8 completion booleans; preserve failures as zeros. Compare paired seed-level candidate-minus-current rates using 10,000 bootstrap seed resamples, Python RNG seed 20261107, percentile endpoints indices 249 and 9749. Completion preservation requires the lower 95% endpoint ≥−0.02 for each of 3 phases × 2 schedules. During sustained impairment, candidate-minus-normal completion must be ≥+0.10 and its lower 95% endpoint >0. Late recovery uses the last 4 of 8 sustained recovery opportunities: compare per-seed average total reserve cost per scheduled opportunity, and require candidate pooled mean ≤0.90 times current pooled mean and paired 95% endpoint for candidate-minus-current cost <0. Healthy cost uses all 8 initial opportunities per seed, verified identical between schedules where histories are still equal; candidate pooled mean must be ≤1.05 times current pooled mean and upper 95% endpoint for candidate-minus-current cost ≤0.05 times current pooled mean. If the current mean cost is zero, the corresponding relative-cost gate is unassessable and fails, not automatically passes. Completion, cost and intervals are reported separately; there is no composite utility. All stated gates must pass. No fresh-seed tuning, substitution of controller, exclusion of failed opportunities or repeated preparation.

Store receipt-bearing final checkpoints and all raw events per history. Validate all 128 expected keys, profile, preparation once before arm cloning, initial neural and RNG equality, and action attribution. At the halfway boundary of every history, restore a typed portable checkpoint and compare the next complete opportunity and resulting full checkpoint to the uninterrupted continuation. A restore must not repeat preparation. These 128 comparisons are in-process; actual process exit and continuation are separately covered by the profile regression and prior CB-02 result. Report seed-level values and paired intervals, action propensity, time to completion with non-completions, outcomes, geometric context, failed/interrupted/blocked/exhausted counts, complete expenditure, CPU and memory limits. If the development gates fail, do not run this fresh panel.
