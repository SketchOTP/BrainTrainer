# CB-20261107-05 prospective complete-action forecast protocol

Freeze this protocol, the emitted condition table, preparation profile, observer, runner, analysis and relevant program/source digests before any fresh case. This panel tests observational prediction by the current adaptive motor controller, not whether the observer improves choices. The comparison and contextual forecasts see the same brain-selected actions and update only after their terminal outcomes. The learned contextual observer is optional and does not choose actions, targets, effort, body physics or interruption. Original brain, inherited preparation and gain-1 host feedback remain unchanged.

Use 16 seed IDs 700–715: a bounded scan of explicit `seed` fields in `runs` and `docs` files under 4 MB found no matches; this does not establish global uniqueness of those numbers. Apply the qualified one-pass age-1 inherited-preparation profile once to each seed, before its 24-opportunity continuous history. Construct the existing adaptive motor controller; retain neural and model state, random state, pending queues, body position and velocity, clock, and reserve across the history. An individual's initial energy is 0.5; its ordinary outcome-based energy changes persist. This profile represents inherited preparation rather than food experience.

Schedule eight healthy, eight sustained-impaired and eight recovered opportunities, with 64 ticks per opportunity. At each opportunity start set the external hunger to the assigned 0.8 or 0.05, then allow ordinary changes within that opportunity; this is controlled test-body input, not a natural hunger cycle, and does not affect the user's live individual. Brain chemical 150 follows the existing host. Set physical strength to 1.0, 0.2 and 1.0 respectively, with existing scheduled obstacle and movement semantics; the predictor never receives strength, phase, seed, assigned food value or future schedule. Offer exactly one existing food identity at distance 2.0 on the assigned left or right side. An action can start later at a different observed distance. A seed's beneficial food identity is `(seed // 2) % 2`; each value assignment occurs for four even and four odd seed IDs. The deterministic generator permutes the full 2 hunger × 2 food × 2 side combinations in each phase per seed; it emits and verifies all 384 rows before fresh execution. Hence value, identity and side are independently counterbalanced within the panel even though the food outcome itself remains hidden until interaction.

Each newly accepted brain-selected eat action produces forecasts before its first execution step, tied to the original action and target, actual remaining deadline and model version. Its completion probability means consumption before the earlier of the host's 32-tick action deadline or the opportunity deadline. Its cost forecast is total reserve expenditure attributable to that original action until termination; matching unique motor-attempt IDs capture failed work as well as successes. Cancellation by an actual changed brain choice and deadline expiration are observed non-completions, not proof of motor incapacity. A host-labeled target-loss or unsupported/rejected execution is unknown/censored; intermediate blocked movement remains an unknown step, not a terminal action or fabricated motor failure. Each terminal updates the relevant estimates at most once. A no-request opportunity produces no action forecast and remains in the behavioral denominator.

The comparator is the observer's per-action-kind and known controller-policy running estimate, with Beta(1,1) completion prior, a two-observation cost prior of 0.5 units, and updates on its own preceding attributable terminal actions. `adaptive` is the currently known execution policy, not the actual future per-step effort; the latter is not available at forecast time. The candidate uses a frozen contextual key comprising action kind, policy, observed distance bin (contact ≤0.85, near ≤2, far >2), prior evidence about normal motor success (unobserved, <0.4, <0.7, otherwise high), and remaining-horizon bin (≤8, ≤16, >16 ticks). Its completion and cost estimates shrink contextual data toward the comparator with mass four. Current hunger, energy, reserve, distance/contact and pre-existing motor estimates are recorded at forecast time; hidden state is excluded. All forecasts, including zero-evidence prior forecasts, are scored when terminal outcome is observable. The exact implementation and model schemas are pinned in the manifest.

For each seed, calculate mean completion Brier score and mean absolute cost error for each predictor across that seed's observable terminal actions. Subtract candidate from comparator so positive means lower candidate error, then average seeds with equal weight and obtain percentile 95% intervals from 10,000 paired seed-cluster bootstrap resamples with RNG seed 20261107 (sorted positions 249 and 9749). Censored outcomes are excluded from conditional scores and counted; they cannot become successes or failures. Action forecasts with no terminal invalidate integration. The 384 opportunity table includes no-request, unsupported actions, interruptions, physical failures, contact and actual consumption. Provide coverage by seed, phase, hunger, identity and side; calibration bins [0,.25), [.25,.5), [.5,.75), [.75,1]; prior-only count; complete/noncomplete/unknown counts; cost error and seed-level raw scores.

Because the handoff specifies no numerical coverage floor, the following local engineering rule is declared **before fresh outcomes**: predictive usefulness is inconclusive unless every seed has at least one scored terminal action, at least 32 scored actions exist in total, and both completion and non-completion occur. Otherwise report available scores without an effectiveness claim. With coverage, completion usefulness requires the lower 95% endpoint for comparator-minus-candidate Brier score to be strictly positive; cost usefulness requires the lower endpoint for comparator-minus-candidate absolute error to be strictly positive. These are separate gates; structural integration can pass while either or both fail. No fresh-panel parameter tuning or repeated held-out panel is authorized by this protocol.

The independent integration tests must show identical underlying brain/body/controller trajectory with the observer enabled or disabled, exactly-once reconciliation and recovery after a real process exit while a forecast and its action are pending. The new observer checkpoint uses a distinct envelope containing the typed prepared host, pending forecasts, terminal IDs and estimator state; restoration never reruns inherited preparation. The panel itself is not 16 process restarts. Raw per-opportunity observations, actions, outcomes and prediction events, final checkpoints, program digest, complete emitted condition table, seeds, costs, CPU and memory boundaries are retained. Source/digest provenance distinguishes this authored extension from the recovered kernel or native engine.
