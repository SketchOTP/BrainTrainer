# Experienced action feasibility protocol

The 1,024-tick v10 ordinary-life observation produced 124 unavailable-action
outcomes and an invalid-action streak of 13 despite coherent restoration. V11
will use at least two attributed failures for the same action/type (or no-target
action) to defer a currently unavailable substrate proposal into an intrinsic hold.
Existing goals and recovery retain priority. Current advertised availability must
override old exclusions. No failed outcome is invented from a proposal or dream.

Compare v10 and explicitly adopted v11 forks of the same continuing checkpoint for
512 actual ticks each with no new teaching or world intervention. Require fewer
unavailable outcomes, no reduction in completed familiar goals, and preserved
history, identity and restoration. Check current availability changes and unmatched
outcomes at the owning boundary. This measures useful narrow self-knowledge, not
general planning or meaningfulness of every quiet moment.

The paired panel also includes the four qualified teaching histories from seeds
151–154, giving five paired starting histories. Each arm runs 512 ticks; no human
availability is renewed. The existing v10 history remains v10 in the comparator,
and the candidate receives an attributed adoption before activity. Test branches
remain separate from their source histories.
