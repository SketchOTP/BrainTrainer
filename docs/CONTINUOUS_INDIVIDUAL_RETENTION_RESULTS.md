# CB-20261107-01: continuous-individual retention result

The frozen [protocol](CONTINUOUS_INDIVIDUAL_RETENTION_PROTOCOL.md) completed all 160 cases: 20 fresh seeds, walking body, both mirrored positions and both counterbalanced food effects, paired between ordinary gain-1 and the persistent gene-7 tract freeze. The numerical immediate-acquisition, delayed-retention, limited-forgetting and mechanical-continuity gates all passed. This result is specific to this structured, in-contact food task, the example walking body, and this narrow control. The earlier reversal benchmark's failure of its ten-point gate remains unchanged.

| Probe | Ordinary aligned first eat target | Frozen aligned first eat target | Paired ordinary minus frozen, 95% seed bootstrap interval |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Immediate | 73/80 (91.25%) | 20/80 (25%) | +66.25 points [56.25, 77.5] |
| After 640 non-food ticks | 71/80 (88.75%) | 5/80 (6.25%) | +82.5 points [70, 93.75] |
| Same interval with real process exit | 71/80 (88.75%) | 5/80 (6.25%) | +82.5 points [70, 93.75] |

The ordinary immediate-to-delayed drop was 2.5 points, with a paired 95% bootstrap interval of [0, 7.5] points. Its upper endpoint meets the predeclared ten-point limit. No requested eat or no target scores zero in the primary aligned-choice metric. The probe is a disposable clone with body energy and hunger set to 0.5; it preserves its source brain, other chemistry, queue, RNG and pending action. Four counterbalanced branches per seed were averaged before seed-level bootstrapping.

The **large advantage is not a pure measure of preference among eat choices**. All 80 probes in each condition attended to a food at least once. Immediately, the ordinary condition requested eat in 73/80 and completed in 73/80; the frozen condition requested eat in 40/80 and completed in 32/80. After 640 ticks, ordinary requested eat and completed in 71/80, while frozen requested eat in 10/80 and completed in 8/80. Thus the treatment also strongly affects whether the brain requests eating. Among requested eat actions, 73/73 ordinary versus 20/40 frozen were aligned immediately, and 71/71 versus 5/10 after the interval. These conditional rates are selected-subset diagnostics, not the preregistered causal comparison. Positive energy was actually delivered in 73 versus 16 immediate and 71 versus 4 delayed completions. Initial training produced 616 ordinary versus 420 frozen completed consumptions, so subsequent experience also differed between conditions. The comparison supports a contribution of persistent tract state to the combined aligned-choice behavior; it does not isolate a stored food-value association from changes in action propensity, chemistry or other learning.

All 160 cases compared normalized complete individual state at 320 and 640 ticks and matched per-tick interval event digests between uninterrupted execution and two successive child processes separated by a real exit. Their delayed and restarted probes also matched. The 160 intervals comprise 102,400 uninterrupted non-food ticks and 62,873 ticks with body movement, with a visible toy and multiple brain-selected actions; the split process executes the same ticks again. The interval may not represent every type of non-food experience. Actual live server shutdown and reopening also retained the same individual's identity and advanced its saved clock. Separate exact-state automated tests cover a brain-selected action in progress and pending feedback; the pending-feedback case was deliberately instrumented. Raw cases store the uninterrupted event digests and the runner's equality result, not both separate continuation snapshots or both sets of raw interval events, so the equality claim is based on the executed runner and cannot be independently reconstructed from the compact case file alone.

The initial combined batch was terminated by SIGTERM after 24 complete cases for an unknown reason. The remaining seeds ran in separate preserved shards using the same frozen runner. The [assembled raw cases](../runs/cb-20261107-01/heldout-complete.jsonl) contain 160 unique expected condition keys and have SHA-256 `60978a8d959de7d1ab637be672f0d1eda873ef178010f830bc3b1767cc45b149`. The [summary](../runs/cb-20261107-01/heldout-summary.json) retains per-seed rates and intervals. The summed parent and child CPU times reported by complete cases were approximately 7,091 and 3,899 seconds, respectively; maximum reported worker peak RSS was 27,984 KiB. This is not peak process-family memory, and the interrupted batch's discarded work is outside those totals.

A focused suite of 51 baseline, food, attribution, gain, checkpoint and replay tests passed.
