# CB-20261107-04 prepared complete-action comparison

The two [development feasibility gates](CB04_PREPARED_DEVELOPMENT_RESULTS.md) passed: 32/32 immediate and 8/8 post-640-tick prepared cases naturally completed, spanning all four seeds and both mirrored layouts. The prepared fresh panel then completed all 128 continuous histories from 16 seeds, two impairment schedules and four controllers. **The overall predeclared comparison failed:** during sustained impairment, confirmation completed 35/128 scheduled interactions versus 24/128 for normal-only, a paired seed-level advantage of **8.59 percentage points** [3.13, 14.84]. Although the interval was above zero, the point estimate did not reach the required **10 points**. A passing interval does not replace the prespecified magnitude requirement. No parameter tuning or second comparison was performed.

## Context and interpretation

Needs and experience were not reset across the sequential histories. In the sustained confirmation branches, mean external hunger at opportunity start fell from **0.4738** in healthy, to **0.0301** in impaired, to **0.00293** in recovery; mean energy rose from **0.7555** to **0.9898** to **0.9977**. This changing state may contribute to fewer later requests, but the experiment does not isolate hunger from phase, food outcomes, neural adaptation or time and cannot establish a cause for the decline. The low 13.28% recovery completion is the denominator-inclusive result, not evidence by itself of a motor fault or forgetting.

The independent [geometry and attribution audit](../runs/self-model/prepared-cb04/fresh-audit.json) confirmed every initial offered food was exactly 2.0 units away, with 1,536 initial positions on each side. At first eat intent, recorded distances ranged from 0.04 to 11.30 units and wait times from 1 to 64 ticks, reflecting movement and time before intent; a later distance difference does not establish broken mirroring. The audit also exposed a **frozen-design limitation**: seed parity assigned both initial food value and mirror, while offered food identity and direction alternated together. Consequently every beneficial food was on the right (1,536 offers) and every harmful food on the left (1,536 offers). Only one food was visible at a time and value was not directly supplied to the brain, but this association could influence later learning and precludes claims about food preference or side generalization. It was not corrected after seeing outcomes; any future test must counterbalance value and side independently.

Known host-labeled blocks and reserve-unavailable events are unknown motor outcomes, not evidence of impaired motor skill. The independent audit counted **34,890 unsupported-choice ticks**, **11,091 measured motor failures** and **4,464 unknown motor attempts** separately. The cause-label assumption for obstacles remains a host limitation. Across candidate sustained phases, the summary records blocks in 29/128 healthy, 71/128 impaired and 95/128 recovery opportunities; blocks may coexist with a later completion and are not removed from scores. The candidate's conditional mean times among completed interactions were 15.67, 21.00 and 28.94 ticks in healthy, impaired and recovery; timeouts remain in the primary denominators rather than being dropped.

## Reproducibility and limits

Each seed was prepared **once before** cloning the 8 scenario/controller histories, using the typed preparation profile and receipt. All 16 processed 29 of 29 expressed records, with 29 matched rehearsal decisions and brain time advancing from tick 0 to tick 58 before body time began. The audit reconstructed and matched all **128 complete initial checkpoints**, including brain, body, RNG, chemistry, input queues, receipt, reserve, obstacle state and model evidence, allowing only declared model schema/condition differences. All **128 typed in-process checkpoint continuations** matched after the mid-history restore and following opportunity, without repeated rehearsal. Separate focused tests exercise actual process exits; these 128 checks are not 128 process restarts. The audit checked **137,707 ticks and 89,907 motor attempts**, including original action/target, unique attempt IDs, pre-action forecasts and recorded costs. It verified all 20 pre-run pinned source/program/evidence digests.
