# Shared activity qualification

Four prospective seeds 151–154 learn an opaque delay-four object through an actual
help request, supplied companion guidance and two observed effects. Each then
forks into a join and decline condition. Report availability explicitly and allow
ordinary activity to produce the invitation; do not inject an invitation or goal.

Join condition: joining records reported participation, not success. A later real
outcome must close the activity with the response and outcome provenance retained.
Decline condition: the creature continues its activity and retains quiet-time
spacing across restart; it must not invite again for 128 actual ticks. Renew
availability every 30 ticks to distinguish response adaptation from absence.
Compare invitation counts over 96 ticks, while both continue familiar activity.

Boundary checks: absent/no verified shared teaching means no invitation; stale
responses fail without mutation; availability expires through actual activity,
not paused wall time or dreaming; reported absence closes an active invitation;
restored continuation preserves state. No partner selection, automatic presence
recognition, detected attention or general relationship acceptance is claimed.
