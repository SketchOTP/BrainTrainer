## Work sequence

1. Retain continuing identity and attributed episodic memory.
2. Integrate learning, self-reflection and capability review into the brain.
3. Let the individual set, revise, pursue and retire goals from its own experience.
4. Integrate dreaming as offline replay and hypothetical rehearsal; test whether
   it improves waking behavior without inventing lived evidence.
5. Let goals select learning, practice, investigation or executable interventions;
   evaluate actual benefits, costs, regressions and identity-preserving updates.
6. Improve that developmental process itself and demonstrate sustained embodied
   companionship. Modify or replace inherited mechanisms where needed.

Each milestone requires behavioral evidence; plumbing tests alone do not establish
learning, digital understanding, autonomy or companionship. Future Android and
physical embodiments retain this individual's continuity contract.
