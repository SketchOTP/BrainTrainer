# Ordinary-life longitudinal observation

Start from an explicit fork of the continuing v10 individual, preserving its
teaching and skills. Run 1,024 actual autonomous ticks in four separate processes
of 256 ticks each, checkpointing every tick. Make no external world changes,
provide no new teaching, and report no human availability. Confirm each restored
start matches the prior final state. Do not merge the branch into the continuing
individual.

Observe activity choice, invalid-action repetition, familiar goal completion,
reserve maintenance, growth of history and restoration. Repeated invalid choices
(eight consecutive unavailable/lost/no-target outcomes) are a specific
investigation trigger, not a passing biological-aliveness score. A repetitive
small world cannot establish diverse interests or sustained companionship.
Record execution cost as diagnostic context; no performance claim is predeclared.
