# Developmental v10: shared teaching affects voluntary invitations

A familiar activity supported by successful companion teaching can now prompt an
invitation to watch together. The invitation points to the supplied guidance, the
real outcome that supported it, the current activity goal and an availability
report. No partner ranking or selection is present: there is one explicit
`companion-person` channel, not recognition of a person's identity or attention.

Availability lasts 64 actual world ticks after an explicit report. Pausing and
dreaming do not advance that clock. A report of absence closes an active invitation.
The creature continues its own activity whether the invitation is answered or not.
Joining records reported participation; only an actual later successful outcome
can produce `completed-with-reported-participation`. This does not prove that a
human watched. Teaching evidence and the later activity outcome have separate
provenance fields.

An ordinary invitation starts a 32-tick spacing interval. Declining changes it to
128 ticks. That preference affects subsequent voluntary communication and survives
restoration. The initial intervals and update rule are authored; this is bounded
adaptation to a response, not learned optimal social timing. Recovery needs take
priority over invitations. Stale responses fail before adding an event.

## Evidence

The prospective protocol used four fresh seeds, each receiving real supplied
teaching that was subsequently supported by two actual effects. Explicit join and
decline branches then ran for 96 ticks with equal availability renewal. All eight
cases passed. Each join branch offered two invitations; each decline branch offered
one. Both completed the originally invited activity. Participation did not become
success on receipt of a response. Restored continuation matched complete state.

Boundary checks establish no invitation without availability or verified shared
teaching, retained quiet-time spacing and later resumption, expiry after actual
activity, no expiry during pause/rehearsal, stale-response rejection, and rejection
of forged invitation evidence on restore. The teaching-memory ablation changes a
test projection explicitly; it is not represented as a valid continuing history.
A real HTTP join followed by a process restart retained participation and later
closed it on the actual activity outcome.

The broad regression run passed all 86 tests in 125.754 seconds. After adding the
HTTP/restart and expiry/provenance cases and extending the decline boundary, all
seven shared-activity tests passed in 18.337 seconds. These sets overlap; their
counts are not additive. A v9 checkpoint continued for 16 ticks with identical
complete state under preserved and current code.

Browser inspection of an explicit test branch showed the invitation, its teaching
basis, the familiar goal, availability report and Join/Not now controls. That test
server was stopped without merging its experiences into the continuing individual.

## Adoption and limits

V10 is now installed in the same visible individual at `http://127.0.0.1:<port>`.
Full prior history, host state, lineage, learned skills and teaching history were
preserved exactly. One attributed implementation-adoption event was added. The
companion remains paused and durable at tick 93 with 105 records. Availability is
unset and there are no invitations yet in its continuing history. Browser and
runtime inspection confirmed the new controls and preserved teaching result.
All 267 pinned original files remain unchanged.

Use **I'm here for a while** to report availability, then run or step the creature.
It may invite participation when it independently returns to a relevant familiar
activity. **Watch together** reports participation; **Not now** defers later
invitations; **Quiet time** reports unavailability. None of these commands orders
the creature to claim success or creates experiences while paused.

Raw panel cases and receipts are under `validation/shared-activity/`; adoption
before-images and runtime proof are under `validation/visible-v10-adoption/`.
This establishes a bounded consequence of shared teaching and responses, not an
enduring relationship, rich personality, person recognition or sustained owner
acceptance. The complete companion goal remains active and incomplete.
