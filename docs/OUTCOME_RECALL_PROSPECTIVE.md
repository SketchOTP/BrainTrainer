# Prospective bounded positive-recall comparison

Freeze before execution: seeds 16–23, each in two independent continuing worlds
with helpful food identity 0 and 1. These seeds are new to this adapter's panel;
no claim of global novelty across earlier brain experiments is made. Mirror is
seed parity, crossed independently with helpful identity. Six alternating offers
(three of each identity), 32 natural ticks maximum per offer, then 24 evaluation
ticks per passive/recall branch. Other resources are hidden during acquisition.
No brain/body reset occurs. The two comparison branches begin from the same full
acquired host state. Recall adoption alone differs, apart from branch metadata.

All acquisition failures and evaluation non-completions stay in denominators.
Primary endpoint: first completed interaction within 24 ticks is with the helpful
food; no completion counts zero. Gate: at least 12 of 16 candidate successes and
positive mean paired improvement, with one-sided exact sign probability <= .05
on non-tied seed-level paired means. Seeds, not the 16 worlds, are the independent
unit. Report counts, ties, harmful consumptions, actual hunger before/after,
response-adjustment totals and reserve expenditure. Acquisition order is still
fixed, and object/category labels are simulator inputs; this is a narrow
qualification of positive recall, not general intelligence or food avoidance.

Archive source and its digest before the first case. Save acquisition and final
branch checkpoints plus raw records. Independently reconstruct endpoint and body
changes from those files. Unit tests and actual process restart remain separate.
No parameters or seeds may be changed after observing this panel. If the gate
fails, retain the failure and revise on development data before a new panel.
