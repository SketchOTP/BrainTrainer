# Spatial self-review, developmental executive v14

The v13 avoidance review assumed the earlier one-dimensional body. It multiplied
horizontal displacement by the target's horizontal offset, even for recorded
2D world movement. Retreat along the vertical axis therefore became a false
stall and could trigger an unnecessary offline rehearsal.

A real-body paired test starts from an experienced harmful-food outcome, then
places the creature beside the eastern forest boundary with the familiar food
north of it. Ordinary avoidance decisions move south. Separate explicit test
branches demonstrate v13 progress=0 and one unnecessary rehearsal after three
successful steps; v14 recognizes the growing observed separation and records
progress=4 after four steps. No successful movement was injected into the trace.
The initial qualification environment placement is explicitly external.

V14 uses before/after perceived target distance, requires actual body movement,
a matched outcome, and a continuous region. Missing target observations or an
unrelated outcome remain insufficient evidence, rather than becoming a failed
attempt. The recorded motion review names its evidence and leaves cause unknown.
A truly immobile body still autonomously rehearses and chooses held actions before
probing again; rehearsals add no lived outcomes or world time.

Old version semantics remain available during history replay. V13 keeps approach
review support, and existing capabilities remain enabled in v14. The new version
is a developer-authored correction to the individual's self-review mechanism,
not an example of the creature autonomously editing this brain code.

Validation: 16 targeted developmental, approach and spatial-review tests passed;
the final four spatial tests additionally use explicit fork histories. Evidence
is in validation/spatial-self-review/. A live checkpoint preflight preserved the
host, lineage and prior events exactly, with only the self-review model field
changing after the proposed adoption. The map and sprite assets are unchanged.

This does not establish general self-understanding, open-ended dreaming, general
software modification, or completion of the End Goal. Live zero rehearsal or code
revision counts alone are not failure: triggering them artificially would not
establish self-directed development. The correction improves whether real
experience warrants rehearsal and how physical progress affects goal review.

Four additional habitat-upgrade/repeated-development tests passed. V14 was adopted by the same live identity and branch at moment 8015 with a full before image and exact preservation receipt. The live Tailscale habitat was visually checked afterward and remained running. No test-branch experiences were merged.
