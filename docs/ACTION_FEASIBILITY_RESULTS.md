# V11: experienced failures change available action choices

A longer ordinary-life run exposed a gap in v10: self-review recorded unavailable
actions but did not change later proposals. Over 1,024 autonomous ticks, the test
branch produced 124 unavailable-action outcomes, including an uninterrupted streak
of 13. It also completed 23 familiar goals. Four separate process phases matched
state at each boundary; 1,130 total records crossed eight history segments. This
establishes bounded continuity, not indefinite lifetime or meaningfulness of every
action. The total-history capacity remains 10,000 records.

V11 projects attributed failures into action-feasibility evidence. After two actual
unavailable-action failures for an observed type/action, another currently
unavailable substrate proposal is deferred to an intrinsic quiet hold. The same
rule covers actual no-target failures. Unmatched outcomes and imagined attempts do
not count. Active learning and maintenance goals retain priority.

Current advertised availability overrides old exclusions. Declared intrinsic rest
and hold capabilities also override old missing-target evidence. Old failure is
not interpreted as permanent incapability. Unknown target-loss circumstances and
not-in-contact failures remain distinct; this mechanism does not pretend to solve
them. Evidence references and the reason for deferral appear in self-review and
the actual executive record.

The 343 avoided attempts became explicit quiet holds. Moving, activated,
not-in-contact and not-completed outcome counts, reserve and completed familiar
goals were unchanged in each pair. Thus the result is less repeated invalid
execution without lost activity; it does not establish more diverse interests,
richer planning or general competence. Much time still consists of movement in a
small room. That remains a substantive next developmental gap.

All prior event prefixes and lineage were preserved. Restored state and reviews
matched. A preserved v10 checkpoint also continued 32 ticks with identical complete
state under previous and current code. The final regression suite passed all 92
tests in 143.473 seconds.

During qualification, the intrinsic-capability override was added after the panel
process had loaded its module. None of the five starting histories had the affected
old no-target evidence, and installed rest/hold actions cannot generate it in these
runs. The final owner was replayed against all 1,821 applicable panel decisions and
matched every selected action and decision record. The final regression suite
also tests that newly available intrinsic actions override older missing-target
failures. This preserves the exact qualification scope instead of implying an
unchanged source throughout execution.

The ordinary-life observation initially used an incorrect unused status name in
its local streak counter. The final count was recomputed from the complete retained
trace using the executor's real `no-target` status; the observed failures were
unavailable-action outcomes. Raw runner, phase logs, checkpoints and correction
receipt remain in `validation/ordinary-life/`. Panel and regression evidence is in
`validation/action-feasibility/`. No performance improvement is claimed.

## Adoption and remaining scope

The same continuing visible individual now runs v11, paused and durable at tick 93.
Its full prior history, host state, lineage, teaching and skills were preserved;
one external implementation-adoption event was added, bringing its ledger to 106
records. Browser inspection retained the same identity, teaching result and paused
state. No test-branch experiences were merged into its history.

The mechanism and two-failure threshold are authored, while the exclusions and
future choices depend on the individual's actual history. This is useful narrow
self-knowledge, not general self-understanding or a creature-authored code change.
All other full-goal requirements remain subject to their own evidence. Richer
ordinary activity, long-life resource management and sustained companionship are
still unfinished.
