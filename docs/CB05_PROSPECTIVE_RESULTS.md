# CB-20261107-05 prospective complete-action prediction results

The frozen prospective test provides **no demonstrated improvement in completion prediction**, but its separately predeclared cost-prediction comparison passes in this bounded task. The candidate is a passive, authored observer on the existing adaptive motor controller; these results do not establish improved action choice, autonomy or complete-task competence. The scientific result is separate from implementation acceptance, including any subsequent fixes for forecast-record metadata.

The [frozen protocol](CB05_PROSPECTIVE_PROTOCOL.md) and [manifest](../runs/self-model/cb05-prospective/manifest.json) were established before 16 fresh seed histories ran. Each seed had 24 opportunities: eight healthy, eight impaired and eight recovery. In each phase the [emitted table](../runs/self-model/cb05-prospective/condition-table.json) exactly crossed two hunger settings, two food identities and both sides. Beneficial identity was also balanced independently across even and odd seed IDs. Hunger was set by the test host at each opportunity start; energy and all neural, controller, action, reserve and historical state otherwise evolved continuously. These are controlled test individuals, not a natural hunger-cycle demonstration. One inherited preparation occurred per seed and no prior food experience was injected.

Both predictors made forecasts on the **same 386 naturally accepted eat actions**. They shared an action-kind/known-policy comparator estimate; the contextual candidate additionally used observable starting-distance, prior normal-motor estimate and actual remaining-deadline bins. Action-specific/attributable reserve consumption, not all spending elsewhere in the opportunity, was the cost target. The known `adaptive` execution policy is available before execution; actual future per-step effort is not a forecast feature. Target identity, initial observations, pre-existing motor statistics, deadline and actual action/target IDs remain in raw records; no hidden food value, body-strength regime, phase or seed enters the estimator's contextual key. No forecasts change the creature's decisions or execution.

For inference each seed's observed actions were averaged first and 16 seed means were resampled together in 10,000 paired bootstraps. All 16 seeds had 21–27 scored actions; there were 386 scored actions in total with both outcomes, meeting the locally declared coverage floor. The cost effect is statistically positive under this protocol; its practical importance is not established. The completion result is not a claim that the two predictors have equal capability in every setting. There were no censored terminal actions in this particular panel; the treatment of unknowns was fixed before the run and cannot be validated by their absence.

The actual terminal records were **280 completed**, **87 interrupted by changed intention** and **19 timed out**. The 384 scheduled opportunities included 280 with a completed consumption. Thirty-eight had no accepted eat action: **11 had no eat choice at all**, while **27 included an eat choice only without a target**. Additionally, 116 opportunities had at least one untargeted eat choice, a count that overlaps other categories. These are not invented physical failures. The panel also recorded 1,948 unsupported-choice ticks, 244 measured motor failures and 497 unknown motor attempts. These event categories use different denominators and can overlap; they must not be added to opportunity totals.

The contextual model had no matching context-specific observations for 100 forecasts. Of those, **16 were truly prior-only** with no global history; the other 84 borrowed the comparator's existing global evidence. The frozen summary's `prior_only=100` label counts absence of *context-specific* evidence; interpreting all 100 as no history would be incorrect. Coverage includes all need/side/phase/identity cells in the [summary](../runs/self-model/cb05-prospective/summary.json). Calibration is descriptive and on-policy: for the candidate's high probability bin [0.75,1], average forecast was 0.836 and actual completion frequency was 0.712 across 184 correlated actions; no formal calibration claim was predeclared.

The independent [raw audit](../runs/self-model/cb05-prospective/audit-v2.json) checked all 21 pinned pre-run source/program/table digests, all 384 emitted scene/need rows, original lifecycle start, target, action and deadline, pre-outcome predictions and evidence counts against an independent reconstruction of the update formulas, terminal reconciliation, unique motor-attempt attribution and costs. It verified 6,877 motor attempts. Per-tick actual strength was not separately sampled in raw events: it is derived from the pinned runner's fixed phase rule and recorded condition; no broader runtime measurement is claimed. No fresh-panel parameter tuning or repeat was performed.

The [raw cases](../runs/self-model/cb05-prospective/cases.jsonl) have SHA-256 `44567ca88999f62b3b683a260cd6cdb395e1e20d87805235cfe6562747aab6a7`. Independent seed-level arithmetic reproduced both paired bootstrap means and intervals. The 16 case CPU times sum to about **307.1 seconds**; the maximum worker peak RSS was **30,984 KiB**, not process-family peak memory, and parallel summed elapsed times are not aggregate wall time. Seed novelty was checked against documented earlier studies and a bounded scan of explicit seed fields under 4 MB; that does not prove global uniqueness across unrelated artifacts. The repository has no Git metadata, so the manifest's content digests, not a commit ID, identify this run.

The frozen observer's per-action forecast has evidence counts and is associated with an outer versioned observer checkpoint and frozen source, but does not carry an explicit self-contained estimator-version or insufficient-evidence flag in each forecast record. This is a structural requirement gap, not a reason to rewrite frozen evidence or claim integration complete. A separate metadata-only correction and bounded replay can be evaluated against the archived original; no new fresh panel is implied. The live-view display and real-process pending-action restore have their own verification and should be reported separately from these held-out prediction results.

After this panel, the frozen observer was archived byte-for-byte and an additive metadata correction added explicit estimator schema/name, prior-only and insufficient-evidence flags to each new forecast. It did not alter the frozen cases or numeric scoring. A [bounded compatibility follow-up](../runs/self-model/cb05-prospective/metadata-replay-v3-receipt.json) independently reran seeds 700 and 707 with the archived and updated observers: the archived reruns reproduced the original raw cases, and all numeric forecasts, host events and resulting states matched after stripping only the added metadata. This checks two previously seen cases, not universal equivalence or a new held-out result. The initial comparison harness rejected a tuple-versus-JSON-list representation before normalization; its exact source and [failure receipt](../runs/self-model/cb05-prospective/metadata-replay-initial-failure.json) are preserved. A second attempt stopped before execution because exclusive-create refused an existing output filename; its source and [receipt](../runs/self-model/cb05-prospective/metadata-replay-v2-failure.json) are also retained. The successful normalized replay has its own source manifest and raw paired case records. A test was added after the original manifest, then the exact frozen test was restored and the new regression retained separately; the producer and model sources used by the actual panel remained unchanged throughout that run.

The optional live integration uses a separate prepared checkpoint and leaves the original running individual untouched. Before a real tick that will accept an eat request, it previews the frozen pre-execution forecast on a disposable copy; the view exposes that preview while the real host is still at its prior tick. Any intervening scene, body or observer change invalidates it. When paused, the first Step can show a preview and the second executes it; automatic mode deliberately waits approximately 1.5 seconds in wall time to expose the preview, without changing brain-tick order. A separate server regression verified the preview before execution, invalidation on a body change, actual process restart with original action and forecast pending, subsequent one-time completion and action journal entries. An in-app view was also exercised through preview, action and matching outcome on an isolated individual. These are implementation observations, not additional held-out prediction data.

Forecasts include explicit model/schema and prior-only/limited-context-evidence metadata after the compatibility-checked correction; the limited-context flag uses an authored threshold of five contextual observations, not a statistical confidence bound. The optional server checkpoints reconciled model state when a forecast or terminal occurs and then appends the event to a lifecycle JSONL. Those are separate files, not an atomic transaction: an abrupt crash between writes can omit a journal event, and the last durable checkpoint remains the authority for restored state. A combined focused selection of **75 tests** passed after the final server changes, including passive host equality, pre-step preview, checkpoint/restart, hidden-input and metadata compatibility tests. Integration passes its tested scoped checks; completion-prediction usefulness does not pass, while the separately declared cost-error gate does.

A separate unforced passivity regression then ran a prepared seed for 64 ordinary brain-selected ticks with and without the observer. Every host event, final complete prepared checkpoint and motor record matched, and at least one real action forecast occurred. This supplements the earlier forced-choice boundary tests; it is not a population-level behavioral equivalence claim.
