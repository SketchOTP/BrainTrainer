# CB-20261107-02 development calibration: qualification stopped

The approach-task calibration did **not** satisfy its minimum completion floor, so no fresh-seed complete-interaction evaluation was run and no task-level success or non-inferiority gate was claimed. The best observed distance was 2.0 units: 4 of 32 scheduled controller cases completed within 64 ticks, below the 8-of-32 floor. All four completions were the *same* development seed 0 / mirrored layout repeated under the four paired controls, with identical brain choices and lifecycle outcomes. They are one of eight distinct seed/layout contexts, not four independent demonstrations. Distances 1.0 and 1.5 produced zero of 32 completions each. These are initial-healthy development trials, not a test under impaired physics.

The [calibration plan](COMPLETE_ACTION_DEVELOPMENT_CALIBRATION.md) specified the distance grid, development seeds, paired controls and selection rule. Before inspecting any outcome values, an 8-of-32 minimum and the use of the full 32 scheduled cases as the budget-rate denominator were added to prevent a vacuous selection from a few successes. The calibration jobs had already started when this additional floor was documented. All failures remain in the scheduled-case denominators. Each case started a fresh individual from one of four development seeds, with one food and no toy in view, both mirror directions, and either normal-only, always-effort, current-adaptive or confirmation execution. Across each distance, only seed 0 ever requested eat: seeds 1–3 attended the offered food during all 64 ticks in each mirrored baseline case but selected other actions. The single successful seed/layout first requested eat at tick 2, interrupted it at tick 3, and later started a second request; the brain retained that request long enough to approach and consume. This establishes a narrow *brain-chosen* example, not general inability, native parity, or a cause for the low request rate. It suggests that task-level qualification is presently limited by action selection and sustained execution, before a meaningful comparison of effort policies is possible.

The representative confirmation-candidate [natural trace](../runs/self-model/complete-action/natural-trace.json) records the whole interaction, brain/body/model snapshots and feedback continuation. For original action ID `46` and target `object-0`, its brain-selected eat action began at tick 47, approached through tick 54, registered contact at tick 55, and consumed at tick 56. The attributed result queued one hunger adjustment of −0.25, which was delivered once on the next tick as `resp[2] = −0.25`. A read-only wrapper observed the queue call; it did not choose the action or alter its feedback. This one trace is not a visible human interaction or a controlled claim that a self-model improved completion.

All four controls followed the same action/lifecycle path in that context. Their entire 56-tick motor reserve expenditures, including unrelated moves and failed or interrupted work, were 0.5 normal-only, 5.0 always-effort, and 1.4 each for current and confirmation. This is one matched healthy context, not a cost-saving estimate or the predeclared task gate. Across all 96 cases there were 6,112 brain ticks, 24 cases requesting eat, 12 reaching contact, four consumptions, 24 cases with interruptions and 72 cases with at least one explicitly labeled environmental block; no case exhausted motor reserve. Those flags can overlap. Summed per-case elapsed time was about 172 seconds across parallel workers; process-family peak memory and aggregate CPU were not measured.

The added focused tests `test_complete_action_calibration.py` passed 2/2: a natural brain-selected consume plus attributed feedback delivery, and a no-request case retained as unsuccessful. The failed dev floor means the planned 16-seed, two-schedule, four-controller fresh study and its completion, resource and noisy-schedule gates are **not assessed**. It would be invalid to infer either candidate superiority or candidate inability from these data. Further work should investigate why the offered food is attended without eliciting eat across these seeds, and why the lifecycle is interrupted, before selecting another task or changing the brain. The developer-defined host action-interruption and collision rules remain unverified as native Docking Station parity.
