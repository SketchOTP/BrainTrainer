# CB-20261107-01: continuous individual retention protocol

Frozen after the single development-seed smoke and before the fresh-seed cases. This is a new scoped experiment; earlier food-preference results and their failed reversal gate remain unchanged. It does not establish a whole-brain learning-disabled control or native-engine parity.

Use walking individuals with gain-1 feedback and the unchanged recovered brain. Compare ordinary operation with the existing persistent gene-7 drive-to-combination tract freeze. The freeze permits transient computation inside each tick and restores that tract's persistent state afterward; other learning remains active. For each fresh seed 132–151, run both mirrored positions and both counterbalanced food values under both conditions, producing 160 cases, four paired layouts per seed. These seeds are outside documented development, previous held-out and half-gain sets; a scan for explicit seed fields in food-preference and qualification artifacts under 4 MB found no matches, which does not establish global uniqueness across unrelated research.

Each individual undergoes 20 presentations, without resetting its body, brain, chemistry, queues, clock or active action. The two foods are placed in contact at ±0.5 from its current position; each presentation ends at a consumption or 32 ticks. They are reoffered by an environmental placement, not an action suggestion. After the last presentation, hide the foods, offer a non-food toy and run one real tick to deliver any outstanding feedback. Then create a disposable immediate probe clone. During the subsequent continuous interval, the individual experiences 640 real ticks with food hidden and the toy moved to alternating sides every 64 ticks. No food feedback is injected during that interval. One continuation runs uninterrupted. A second continuation runs its first 320 ticks in one child process, writes a portable checkpoint and exits; another process restores it and completes 320 ticks. Compare complete normalized state at 320 and 640 and the per-tick event digests, then compare delayed probes. Record actions and movement during the interval. This does not reset the live individual.

Immediate, delayed and restarted probes are disposable clones of their respective individual state. Standardize only body energy and hunger to 0.5 and food placements to the same in-contact ±0.5 arrangement, with the toy hidden; retain neural state, chemistry, queues, RNG, pending feedback and pending action. Observe up to 32 ticks. The primary aligned-choice score is 1 if the first brain-requested eat target is the higher-value food, otherwise 0, including no request. Attention, requested eat, completed consumption, and beneficial outcome are reported separately. The first requested target need not complete; this is an aligned-choice study, not a success-by-consumption rate. Probe feedback follows actual execution on the disposable clone and cannot reach the source individual.

Average the four counterbalanced branches per seed, then compute ordinary minus frozen paired seed differences. Bootstrap 20 seed pairs with replacement 10,000 times using random seed 20261107, with percentile endpoints at indices 249 and 9749 of the sorted means. Immediate and delayed each require an advantage of at least 0.10 and a lower 95% interval endpoint above zero. Limited forgetting requires the upper 95% endpoint for ordinary immediate minus ordinary delayed to be at most 0.10. Restarted states and continuation events must match exactly. These are gates for this experiment only, not reinterpretations of earlier benchmarks.

The smoke used development seed 0 (both mirrors, assignments and conditions); all eight continuations matched and 40 training consumptions occurred. It is a functional check, not confirmatory evidence. The runner's child-CPU accounting was corrected after the smoke; smoke CPU totals are not comparable to the frozen runner. The manifest records source and program digests before fresh cases. Raw cases preserve every training completion and every probe tick; interval event digests and state equality verify continuation without storing every interval tick. CPU and worker peak memory are reported as bounded process measurements, not full process-family peak memory.
