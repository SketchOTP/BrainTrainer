# Rest and continuing activity protocol, v1

Frozen before prospective seeds 101–104. Begin with the same prepared individual,
development enabled, an unfamiliar switch and an observed low motor reserve of
0.03. Body changes are attributed environmental interventions. Fork explicit test
branches: v6 rest-capable development and the previous v5 implementation without
rest recovery. Record the implementation difference in the control branch. Run
256 ordinary ticks without manually refilling reserve or assigning actions.

Measure actual displacement (sum of absolute position changes), rest outcomes,
reserve trajectory, completed learning goals and final continuing-state equality
after restoration. The v6 individual must autonomously rest, recover at least
once, learn the switch's effect, and perform more actual movement than its v5
counterpart. Reserve may not become negative or exceed 100. Rest evidence must
refer only to real rest outcomes, not dreams or pauses. The comparison establishes
the contribution of the authored rest capability and maintenance drive together;
it does not isolate which component supplies the benefit.

The initial drive rests below reserve 1 and resumes ordinary goals at reserve 3.
The body restores 0.5 reserve per actual rest tick, capped at 100. These quantities
are authored physical/instinct parameters, not discoveries. The expected recovery
value is learned only from prior real outcomes. Three nonrecovering rest outcomes
must stop the failed method and expose a need for investigation; they cannot count
as recovery. Targetless rest must work in an empty environment. Dedicated tests
cover a zero-recovery body, restart during rest and no recovery during pause/dream.

This is not nutrition, sleep science, general self-awareness or long-term
companionship. The finite ledger capacity and other ordinary-life gaps remain.
The existing visible v5 individual is preserved without silently upgrading its
body or development model.
