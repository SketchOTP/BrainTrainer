# Creature Brain: working goal and acceptance reference

Adopted from the owner's full “Creature Brain — End Goal & Definition of Success”
supplied on 2026-11-08. This is a concise implementation reference; the supplied
specification remains authoritative. Authorization is to pursue the complete goal.

Build one persistent digital individual from the recovered Creatures brain: a
consequential digital body, experience-driven development, an enduring human
relationship, and useful understanding of its own software. The intended result
is a believable Digimon-like companion with initiative, individuality and shared
history. DefaultBrain is the foundation, not a permanent ceiling.

Experience → learning → self-understanding → self-directed improvement → executable
changes → retained growth → better future learning and companionship.

## Owner clarification: developmental brain first

The owner clarified that the recovered Docking Station brain is starting material
for our own brain, not an architecture that must be preserved or optimized for
its own sake. Learning, memory, self-reflection, dreaming, self-review, and the
ability to set and update its own goals are central to self-improvement. Alter
or replace inherited mechanisms when the end goal requires it. Partner selection
is not a required creature-brain capability and is removed from active scope.
The relationship and companionship ambition does not imply ranking people.
Preserve individual continuity and prior evidence through implementation changes.

## Required capabilities and evidence

1. **Continuity:** retain identity, meaningful history, acquired abilities,
   preferences and relationships across actual process restarts and qualified
   updates. Preserve unfinished activities where possible. Pauses add no imagined
   experience; divergent copies have explicit branching history.
2. **Embodiment:** meaningful perception, movement, contact, interactions and
   observable failures. The reusable brain is host-independent. Android camera,
   microphone and speaker are later interfaces; neither those nor a future robot
   body may be prerequisites for identity or basic virtual life.
3. **Initiative:** choose, persist with, revise and resume meaningful activities;
   explore, play, practice, maintain, rest and seek help when useful. Experience
   increasingly changes selection and execution. Constant optimization is not
   the mission.
4. **Learning:** retained changes affect decisions, adapt to changed conditions,
   and generalize with appropriate uncertainty. Distinguish experienced events,
   inherited preparation, supplied information, hypotheses, simulations and
   implementation changes. Descriptive recollection alone is insufficient.
5. **Individuality:** coherent, stable but changeable tendencies develop from
   differing histories. Expressive state and durable personality are distinct.
6. **Relationship:** shared experience causally changes voluntary interaction,
   recognition, teaching, invitations, help requests and appropriate independence.
   A relationship score alone is insufficient.
7. **Grounded communication:** gestures, sound, animation or language reflect
   actual attention, intentions, events, abilities and uncertainty. A language
   model may express the individual but cannot substitute for its continuing state.
8. **Useful self-knowledge:** distinguish not choosing, missing targets, failed
   execution, goal changes, assistance and unknown causes. Knowledge must improve
   choices among trying, practicing, investigating, changing strategy and help.
9. **Digital understanding:** operationally distinguish world action, learned
   state and executable changes; self and test copies; pause, restart, rollback,
   migration and external intervention. Demonstrate correct predictions and choices.
10. **Self-directed development:** identify limitations and choose an intervention
    through ordinary continuing activity; evaluate, stop or revise ineffective
    approaches and retain worthwhile changes.
11. **Executable improvement:** repeatedly initiate a problem, define observable
    success, diagnose, choose practice or software intervention, generate or
    commission code, test isolated candidates and regressions, reject failures,
    retain worthwhile changes, and continue with history and abilities intact.
    Developer-directed patches are not evidence of creature-directed improvement.
12. **Improve improvement:** separately demonstrate better learning, practice
    selection, uncertainty, diagnosis, candidate generation/evaluation or knowledge
    preservation. A single successful patch does not establish this.

## Integrated success

One continuing individual must demonstrate all twelve, plus believable
companionship over sustained use. Controlled evaluations and owner experience
both matter. Balance competence, learning, continuity, individuality, relationship,
initiative, communication, reliability and resource cost. Engagement, patch counts
or a food score cannot replace this definition.

The target scenario includes encountering novelty, learning, seeking useful help,
identifying a persistent implementation limitation, rejecting a regressive
candidate, accepting a beneficial candidate, resuming ordinary companionship and
transferring improvement knowledge to a different problem.

Recursive improvement is a long-range ambition, not a guarantee of unlimited or
accelerating intelligence. Subjective consciousness is unresolved and separate
from functional capability evidence. No claim about external research is required
for local implementation acceptance.

## Owner clarification: reflective LLM, lived trials and growing animation

The owner explicitly authorized an external harness using the installed Codex
CLI with OAuth. The LLM is intended as part of the individual's internal
reflection: self-review, analysis, dreaming, goal development and improvement,
not merely an external programmer. Persistent identity and experience remain
authoritative across its calls. Generated interpretations are not lived facts.

Learning must be tested through the creature's actual actions in its continuing
habitat. A proposed lesson needs observable success/failure and a stopping or
reconsideration condition. Thinking, dreams or hidden branch results cannot
stand in for lived practice. Isolated software checks may detect crashes before
installation; usefulness still needs continuing-world experience.

The habitat also needs built-in generation of new sprite animations for new
activities, preserving the supplied creature design and world visual style.
Generated clips must be tied to actual activity/state and visually qualified.
The animation library must be extensible; new art does not itself establish
that an ability has been acquired.

The owner also requires an environment agent that can add and update interactable
objects and their visual states in the same world art style. Creature interaction
must cause real environment changes (for example harvesting, visible depleted
plants and later regrowth), with matching collision, affordances, resources and
learning evidence. New images alone do not fulfill this requirement. Environment
updates and creature animation must describe the same authoritative action and
world state.
