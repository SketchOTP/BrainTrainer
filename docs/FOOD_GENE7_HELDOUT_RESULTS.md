# Held-out gene-7 in-contact comparison

The predeclared held-out test completed all **640 cases**, using seeds 56–95, walking and swimming, mirrored positions, both food-value assignments and paired ordinary versus gene-7 persistent-tract-state freeze. Each case had 20 opportunities in each of four phases and a 32-tick opportunity limit. A missed or failed interaction counts as unsuccessful. The test asks whether retaining this tract state improves performance under the experimental in-contact action lifecycle; it is not a whole-brain learning-disabled comparison or native action-parity test.

For each body and evaluation phase, the primary comparison averages the four mirror/assignment branches within each seed, then compares the 40 paired seed scores. Intervals below are the frozen 10,000-resample paired-seed percentile bootstrap. Each body/control/phase includes 3,200 opportunities. The preregistered target required a difference of **at least 10 percentage points and an interval entirely above zero in both initial and reversal evaluations for each body**.

| Body and phase | Ordinary | Gene-7 frozen | Paired difference | 95% interval | Target |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Walking, initial evaluation | 25.13% | 12.03% | +13.09 pp | +10.47 to +15.84 pp | Met |
| Walking, reversal evaluation | 19.22% | 10.69% | +8.53 pp | +5.31 to +11.81 pp | Not met |
| Swimming, initial evaluation | 24.56% | 11.72% | +12.84 pp | +10.25 to +15.44 pp | Met |
| Swimming, reversal evaluation | 17.69% | 10.41% | +7.28 pp | +4.53 to +10.19 pp | Not met |

**The overall four-part target was not met:** both initial evaluations passed, while both reversal point estimates fell below ten percentage points. All four intervals were above zero. This held-out result supports a contribution of persistent gene-7 state to the measured task performance in this executor, including a smaller positive reversal effect. It does not by itself identify a pure food-value representation: the conditions generate different later choices, sensory histories and feedback, and other retained neuron and tract states remain active.

Non-completion remained common. In initial evaluation, ordinary versus frozen walking completed 921 versus 770 of 3,200 opportunities, and swimming 901 versus 750. In reversal evaluation the corresponding completions were 737 versus 684 for walking and 686 versus 666 for swimming. As a secondary selected-subset diagnostic, ordinary versus frozen higher-value fractions among completed consumptions were 804/921 versus 385/770 for walking initial, 615/737 versus 342/684 for walking reversal, 786/901 versus 375/750 for swimming initial, and 566/686 versus 333/666 for swimming reversal. These conditional rates do not replace the primary scores.

The matched-needs probe clones each branch, clears pending outcome feedback on the clone, fixes observed geometry and needs, then observes one brain tick without executing the action. After initial experience, ordinary/frozen chose eat at the higher-value target in 99/160 versus 14/160 walking branches and 100/160 versus 8/160 swimming branches. After reversal experience the figures were 66/160 versus 15/160 and 64/160 versus 13/160. These 160 branch observations per cell share 40 seeds. Other retained neuron activity, chemistry and RNG were not reset; the probes are retention-sensitive observations, not proof that the transplanted tract alone encodes food value. The separate [controlled transfer diagnostic](FOOD_TRACT_TRANSFER_DIAGNOSTIC.md) directly tested tract-state influence in four development seeds and one body.

All eight shard receipts, raw-case hashes and the frozen [manifest](../runs/food-qualification/gene7-heldout-v1-manifest.json) were checked. The [analysis artifact](../runs/food-qualification/gene7-heldout-v1-analysis.json) validated all 640 unique cases, full paired initial snapshots apart from the intended control marker, opportunity phase alignment and recorded persistent gene-7 state. All producer midpoint checkpoint-continuation checks passed; held-out continuations were not independently rerun. Four development cases were independently replayed from saved initial checkpoints through reconstructed midpoints, as documented in [the replay report](FOOD_INDEPENDENT_REPLAY.md). The original midpoint checkpoints were not retained.

The study was paused by the user after the first shard and 48 cases of the second shard; the second shard resumed using its bound start receipt and validated retained rows. Summed case CPU time was approximately 99,281 seconds. The maximum cumulative per-worker peak RSS reported by the cases was 328,076 KiB; it is not process-family peak memory. Total wall time across the pause and resumed shards is not represented by a single continuous measurement. The experimental action interruption rule remains unverified against a competing brain-selected action in Docking Station; native approach and touch evidence is linked from [the lifecycle report](FOOD_ACTION_LIFECYCLE.md).
