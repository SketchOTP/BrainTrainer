# Partner-history development protocol

Before this panel: seeds 32–35, each with both assignments of arbitrary actor
labels `alex` and `zuri` to familiar/newcomer roles. The roles are evaluator labels,
not learner inputs. All worlds retain the fixed food-0 helpful/food-1 harmful
body rule; six initial alternating offers give the familiar person three helpful
outcomes and a separately identified system three harmful opportunities. A new
person offers one helpful item. Every offer allows 32 natural ticks; failures
remain recorded. Hunger and brain state are never reset.

With no visible food, compare the legacy request to explicit adoption of the new
relationship component in the same continuing individual. The candidate should
request the familiar person despite the lexical label order. A system actor is
not eligible; an actor whose presence is unknown is not assumed available.
Mark the familiar person absent: the newcomer should become the recipient.
Restore presence, then expose four actual harmful offers from the familiar person.
The candidate should revise its preference to the newcomer. Hide all known
people: the candidate should abstain. No messages are sent to anyone.

The authored score is recent mean observed response relief multiplied by n/(n+2).
It is a decision heuristic, not a probability or a claim about human character.
Presence and person/system classifications are supplied observations, not face
recognition. Requests are internal communicative intentions; this panel cannot
establish an enduring relationship or useful real-world assistance. Report every
phase, offered/completed counts, outcomes and evidence references. Preserve actual
process-restart coverage separately and preserve the earlier recall qualification.
