# Outcome recall results

The prospective panel passed its predeclared **first helpful interaction** gate:
16/16 recall branches versus 9/16 passive branches completed their first
interaction with the helpful food. Across eight seed-level paired means, seven
improved, none worsened and one tied; the one-sided exact sign probability was
0.0078125. Mean paired improvement was 0.4375. This is a bounded sensory-recall
result, not whole-brain learning isolation, general intelligence or companionship.

The 16 continuous acquisition histories completed 93/96 offered interactions.
Three unsuccessful offers remained in the records and denominators. Helpful food
identity and side were crossed independently. Acquired brain/body state was
shared at each passive/recall split. The recall path only reads observed events
and present observations; the hidden world value is used by the evaluator, not
by the learner. The primary endpoint and seeds were frozen before execution.

| Evaluation measure | Passive | Recall |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| First completed interaction helpful | 9/16 | 16/16 |
| Harmful consumptions | 15 | 16 |
| Mean actual body hunger change | -0.0234375 | -0.125 |
| Mean motor reserve spent | 0.444375 | 0.28375 |

**Harm avoidance is not established.** Every recall branch later consumed the
harmful food. The earlier v2 development candidate supplied retreat cues but the
brain kept choosing eat in all four histories. That additional policy was
rejected, its code removed from the working candidate, and its source and full
results retained. These rejections were made by the development process, not by
the creature; they do not establish self-directed software improvement.

Memory affects real decisions through existing noun/verb sensory inputs; the
recovered brain still chooses every action and target. Its authored value model
learns from original executed-action attribution. The feedback hunger adjustment
is a response signal; the report separately computes actual body hunger change,
which can differ under clipping. Unfamiliar object types/categories receive no
transferred expectation. Reversal is covered only by synthetic boundary checks,
not this behavioral panel. Fixed offer order, contact geometry, a short horizon,
and the simplified body's limited activities constrain the result.

An exploratory assistance audit found all request evidence linked to actual
positive outcomes and attributed offers. All 16 histories generated requests.
In eight histories, requests initially included `evaluation-runner` after its
helpful offer, rather than only the previously familiar person. This follows the
current equal-mean, lexical tie rule and exposes a relationship limitation: actor
labels are supplied identifiers, not recognized people, and one helpful offer can
tie a longer history. No enduring-relationship claim follows from these requests.
Requests are internal intentions, not messages sent to people.
