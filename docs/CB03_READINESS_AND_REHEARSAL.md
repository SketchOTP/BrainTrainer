# CB-20261107-03: readiness and rehearsal evidence

The CB-02 calibration cases start newly constructed walking individuals at age stage 1, time zero, external hunger 0.8 and energy 0.5. Their brains contain 29 expressed instinct records from 33 in the underlying program; `instinct_cursor` and `instincts_processed` start at zero, `last_rehearsal` is absent, the 256 brain chemical values are zero, and pending lobe inputs are empty. The default constructor initializes component states and dendrites, but does not call `train_instincts` or `rehearse_next_instinct`. The calibration uses the continuous individual host, whose ordinary tick delivers any pending response input and supplies food category to vision at 0.5, hunger to `driv[2]`, and hunger as chemical 150. The host retains all other brain chemicals; from the all-zero start and without external chemistry updates, they remain zero. The body energy field is not automatically a full biochemical state or a chemical-4 glycogen reading.

The earlier CB-01 task had materially different preparation: 20 food presentations and a real tick to deliver final pending feedback, followed by a disposable immediate probe clone. The probe set external energy and hunger to 0.5 and presented both category-3 and category-8 foods within contact; its first frame supplied both `visn` cells at 0.5, `driv[2]=0.5` and chemical 150 = 0.5. It retained learned neural state, chemistry apart from the next overwritten hunger channel, queued inputs, RNG and active action. Its acquisition can include no completions; do not assume 20 meals. Neither CB-01 nor CB-02 invokes instinct rehearsal. The separate `Simulation.make_demo` host optionally performs a declared boot rehearsal and calls `rehearse_next_instinct` during its modelled REM state; that different host is not used by either experiment.

The contrast includes prior actual food experience, food count, sensory categories, body needs, contact and initial neural state, as well as the different seed ranges. It does not by itself establish which difference caused the CB-02 non-request. Manual or native references to sleep are not evidence that this reference host has a sleep scheduler or ran native sleep; the reconstructed `train_instincts` method is available but not automatic. The original [native report](../research/docking-station-20261103/REPORT.md) identifies a source-derived instinct process and packaged script corroboration but says actual sleep timing was not observed live.

## One input-identical, instrumented pair

The retained CB-02 seed 1, mirrored, distance-2, current-adaptive case has 64 events with category-3 food visible, external hunger 0.8 and no pending food outcome at every tick. In a brain-only diagnostic, both branches began from the same seed-1 age-1 initialization and received the exact same 64 recorded input frames: `visn[3]=0.5`, `driv[2]=0.8`, chemical `150=0.8`, with the other 255 chemicals zero. No body action or food outcome was executed in either branch. The control exactly reproduced all 64 recorded attention, decision and mapped-action winners; it never chose decision 4 / action 12. Its decision-winner counts were 11→action 10 (left): 21; 10→action 9 (rest): 23; 12→action 11 (right): 14; 1→action 1 (push): 5; and 3→action 7 (drop): 1. These mapped actions are reported separately from their decision-neuron IDs.

The intervention used the separate demo host's explicit **boot procedure** once: set chemical 213 to 1, call `train_instincts()` over all 29 expressed records, set chemical 213 back to 0, and clear activity. All 29 records produced a matched-decision training step and advanced the brain 58 internal ticks. Immediately before replay its chemical vector and pending lobe inputs were zero, like the control, but its RNG, learned connections, other neural state and rehearsal cursor had changed. It selected decision 4 / mapped action 12 in all 64 identical input frames. This supports a causal effect of the **bundled initialization procedure in this one reconstruction case** on decision output. It does not identify a particular instinct or changed state, establish that an actual creature would consume food, reproduce native sleep timing, or justify treating untrained creatures as defective. In particular, the constant input frames ignore any changed observations and outcomes that a real body would have produced.

The [raw paired artifact](../runs/self-model/action-init-20261107-03/rehearsal-pair-seed1.json) contains initial, post-preparation and final complete brain states, all input and chemical frames, rehearsal metadata, 64 decision activity vectors and outputs. The [runner](../runs/self-model/action-init-20261107-03/rehearsal_pair.py) and [post-run digest receipt](../runs/self-model/action-init-20261107-03/rehearsal-pair-receipt.json) preserve the method and hashes; these are not a pre-run cryptographic freeze. This is one of the directive's maximum three targeted pairs.

## Second natural-body diagnostic

A separate single pair used seed 1, current-adaptive motor execution, the same initial brain/body/model/RNG, and one food at distance 2.0 in the mirrored direction. The one-pass boot rehearsal was the only setup intervention; after it, each branch made its own unchanged brain choices and executed the ordinary action lifecycle for at most 64 ticks. No action or outcome was injected. The unrehearsed control never requested eat or reached contact and timed out at tick 64. The rehearsed branch selected decision 4 / action 12 targeting `object-0` at tick 1, approached for four ticks, made contact at tick 5 and consumed at tick 6. It generated one pending food-feedback event, which was cleared on an additional ordinary tick. The ordinary host code routes that event into the brain; this pair did not separately instrument the queue call. Motor costs in these individual runs were 0.97 and 0.22 units, respectively; they are not a comparative performance estimate.

The result demonstrates a natural complete interaction for this one seed under that explicit initialization. Later visual, bodily and feedback histories may diverge once choices differ; this is separate from the first input-identical neural comparison. It cannot justify changing default construction, treating 29 genes as individually causal, generalizing a readiness criterion to every creature, claiming native sleep parity, or bypassing CB-02's failed qualification. The [natural-pair artifact](../runs/self-model/action-init-20261107-03/rehearsal-natural-pair-seed1.json) includes initial/post-preparation/final full checkpoints and all opportunity events; its SHA-256 is `670f68be8ac01db8b2100bfacf67169edfaa9cc9b0138e7e7c785690c7b287ee`. The [runner](../runs/self-model/action-init-20261107-03/rehearsal_natural_pair.py) records the intervention and its limits. This uses the directive's final permitted paired replay.

## Proposed uniform preparation for a later qualification

If the next experiment is specifically intended to evaluate an instinct-prepared reference creature, declare the following **before selecting seeds or inspecting outcomes**, and apply it to **every** enrolled seed and every motor-control condition:

1. Construct the same owner-supplied program at age stage 1 and sex with which this diagnostic was performed, using that seed and the ordinary constructor. Save or hash the complete initialization state.
2. On that same brain, perform the existing separate demo's boot sequence: set chemical 213 to 1, run one pass over **all 29 age-stage-1 expressed instinct records**, set chemical 213 to 0, and call `clear_activity`. Do not offer food or select/force a motor action during this procedure.
3. Record the program digest, seed, age/sex, expressed-record count, cursor, processed count, matched-decision count, brain tick and RNG before/after, chemistry, pending inputs, and complete prepared state or a verifiable checkpoint hash. Require that the declared procedure completes, not that it produces an eat winner.
4. Attach the same external body/world, needs, observations and outcome rules to all arms. Keep every enrolled seed, including those that do not subsequently request eat, interrupt, time out or do not complete. Pair the control and candidate on seed and initial state; let their later histories evolve naturally.

This is a **proposed, unqualified research setup**, not a finding that one pass is the native sleep sequence, that all 29 records are expressed in other ages/programs, that any particular gene caused the behavior, or that the procedure makes every seed ready. The one paired natural case and one frozen-input pair above are insufficient to establish generality, so any fresh panel still needs a frozen protocol and all original non-request/completion denominators. An untreated-brain condition can be separately reported if the causal contribution of this bundled preparation is a question; it should not be pooled with a prepared condition. Existing DefaultBrain initialization, gain-1 feedback, the prior baseline checkpoint and CB-02's failed calibration remain unchanged.
