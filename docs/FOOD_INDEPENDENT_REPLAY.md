# Independent reconstruction and continuation replay

The producer of the gene-7 development experiment saved each branch's initial and final checkpoints, recorded every tick, and tested a midpoint restore internally. It **did not retain the original midpoint checkpoint**. Therefore, this replay cannot directly reopen the producer's original midpoint. It independently restores the retained initial checkpoint, reproduces the recorded timeline to opportunity 40, serializes a newly reconstructed midpoint, restores that checkpoint, and checks both the uninterrupted and restored continuations against the retained records and final checkpoint.

The four cases were selected before replay by source, seed, body and control rather than outcome: seed 0, walking, non-mirrored, food-value assignment 0, ordinary and gene-7-frozen, from the original file; and seed 15, swimming, mirrored, food-value assignment 1, ordinary and gene-7-frozen, from the recovered file. These are development cases, not a random sample or held-out experiment.

All four replays passed. Across all recorded ticks, brain choices and targets, lifecycle events and outcomes, pending feedback, queued lobe inputs, chemistry, RNG and each tract's weak queue matched the retained records. At opportunity 40, the reconstructed JSON checkpoint was restored; all later choices and outcomes and the complete final checkpoint matched both the uninterrupted reconstruction and the saved original. The final checkpoint comparison covers learned tract state, neuron state, queues, world and pending state. The recorded midpoint steps were 908 for the seed-0 cases and 1,210 for the seed-15 cases; the four continuations contained 1,033, 1,033, 1,133 and 1,020 ticks respectively.

The [machine-readable replay receipt](../runs/food-qualification/independent-continuation-replay-v1.json) records cases, tick counts, file digests and processing cost. Both retained case-file hashes matched the earlier combined analysis before replay. This is independent re-execution of four captured trajectories with the same implementation, not native Docking Station verification or proof for every branch. It verifies a reconstructed midpoint against the complete recorded trajectory, not byte identity with the unretained original midpoint.

A separate [targeted reversal trace](../runs/food-qualification/reversal-good-feedback-trace-v1.json) reconstructed the first successful consumption of the newly valuable food in the preselected seed-0 walking ordinary branch. The pending feedback retained original eat action `42:0`, target `object-1` and hunger adjustment −0.25. On the following tick, response neuron 2 variable 0 changed from 0 to −0.25, and drive neuron 2 variables 3 and 7 changed from 0 to −0.25 and 0.25. Two gene-7 dendrites changed, including one endpoint change from `(0, 122)` to `(2, 168)`; the weak queue changed from `[1, 2, 3, 4, 5]` to `[2, 3, 4, 5, 6]`. The next selected action was action 8 targeting `object-0`. These are observations during one reconstructed tick with all normal inputs and updates; they do not isolate feedback as the cause of every concurrent change or establish the functional meaning of either connection.
